## Per-halaqa data

Eight halaqat, but **ten level groups**. The sixth halaqa (سعيد بن زيد) has two pages, one for المستوى السادس and one for المستوى الثامن. The eighth halaqa (أبي بكر الصديق) also has two pages, one for المستوى التاسع and one for المستوى العاشر. Each page carries the same halaqa title («الحلقة السادسة» / «الحلقة الثامنة»).

Every halaqa name is followed on the page by «- رضي الله عنه -». Units are printed as «طالب/طلاب» and «وجه».

The teacher, halaqa name, supervisor and level are **identical in A and B**. Only the counts and syllabi changed.
